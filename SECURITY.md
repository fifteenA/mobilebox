# Security

Report vulnerabilities in Mobilebox (the API, the MCP server, the website or this repository) to **hello@mobilebox.dev** with "security" in the subject. Do not open a public issue.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it and what an attacker could do with it. We confirm receipt and tell you when it is fixed.

In scope: authentication and key handling, access to another workspace's phones or data, escape from a phone sandbox to the host or to other phones, and leaks of credentials or screen content.

Please test only against your own account and phones, don't degrade the service for others, and give us reasonable time to fix before you publish.

Leaked a Mobilebox key yourself? Revoke it in the [dashboard](https://mobilebox.dev/dashboard) first, then create a new one.
